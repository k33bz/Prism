Rainbow-Arc ★ hero · shapes-rainbow-arc-hero
Text Shapes · Arcs & Rainbows

The signature piece: each letter is rotated around a hidden center to bow the word into a rainbow arc, every glyph runs its own hue cycle while the whole arc slowly hue-rotates — a true moving spectrum. Pure per-letter CSS, no images. Letter positions are set by a tiny init script.
