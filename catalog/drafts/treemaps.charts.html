.chx-tm-voro
Polygonal cells partition the frame by area, fade in, and a highlight walks between them. Use it for organic part to whole.

.chx-tm-icicle
Layered rectangles draw outward level by level, each row a tier of the tree. Use it to read depth and breadth at once.

.chx-tm-delta

.chx-tm-heat

Treemap Sparklines
.chx-tm-spark
Each cell holds a small trend line that redraws in place. Use it when a treemap slice also needs its recent trajectory.
Hierarchical Bar
.chx-tm-hbar
Parent bars give way to their child bars, which grow into place, then collapse back. Use it to drill a ranked breakdown.
Dendrogram to Bar
.chx-tm-dend
Tree links draw from the root, then resolve into a ranked bar list on the right. Use it to turn a cluster into an order.
Euler Diagram
.chx-tm-euler
Three sets overlap by proportional area and breathe gently so shared regions read. Use it for membership across groups.
Icon Array
.chx-tm-iconarr
A ten by ten dot grid fills left to right to a share, here 63 in 100. Use it to make a percentage feel concrete.
Diverging Likert Bar
.chx-tm-likert
Five point responses stack out from a zero line, disagree left and agree right. Use it for survey sentiment per item.
Semi Donut Needle
.chx-tm-needle
A half donut split into zones with a needle that swings to the reading and settles. Use it for a single rate against bands.
Tree Ring Chart
.chx-tm-treering
