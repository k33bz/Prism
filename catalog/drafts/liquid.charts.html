Pipe Cross Section
.chx-lq-pipe-lvl
A pipe seen end on with a sloshing two layer surface and a percent that counts up as the level rises. Use for a fill or capacity reading.
Liquid Tank
.chx-lq-tank-lvl
A rounded tank whose level rises with a wave surface past graduated ticks and a dashed target line. Use for a level against a goal.
Liquid Ring
.chx-lq-ring-flow
A torus channel where fluid streams around the loop with bubbles carried along at two speeds. Use for a circulating or throughput reading.
Liquid Pipe Run
.chx-lq-run-flow
A horizontal pipe with fluid streaming left to right past a turning valve, speed reading the value. Use for a flow rate or pipeline stage.
Liquid Drop Gauge
.chx-lq-drop-lvl
A teardrop vessel filling from the base with a wave surface inside the drop outline. Use for a humidity, moisture or fill reading.
Liquid Battery
.chx-lq-cell-lvl
Three cells that flood with fluid one after another in a staged sequence. Use for a battery, capacity or multi stage fill.
Dual Tank Compare
.chx-lq-da
Two tanks whose levels settle to actual and target so the shortfall shows as the gap. Use to compare a value against its plan.
Liquid Thermometer
.chx-lq-therm-lvl
A bulb and column where the fluid rises with a rippling top surface against a small scale. Use for a temperature or intensity reading.
Range Area Band
.chx-lq-band
A shaded min to max band fades in, then a median line draws through the middle. Use to show a spread with its central trend.
Fan Chart
.chx-lq-fanwrap
A forecast band opens from the present and widens into three confidence tiers over time. Use for a projection with uncertainty.
Sigma Band Outliers
.chx-lq-out
A mean line inside a one sigma band, with points outside the band pulsing red. Use to flag readings beyond normal variation.
Error Bars On Columns
.chx-lq-whisk
Columns grow to their value, then whiskers extend above and below to show the margin. Use for measured values with uncertainty.
Deviation Area
.chx-lq-devpos
A series wipes in against a baseline, tinting green above and red below the zero line. Use to show gains and shortfalls versus a norm.
Variance To Plan
.chx-lq-vcol
Actual columns rise against a plan line, coloured over or under, with a running variance strip below. Use for budget or target tracking.
Bollinger Envelope
.chx-lq-boll
A moving average with upper and lower bands hugging a jittery price line as it draws in. Use for volatility or range tracking.
Control Limits Band
.chx-lq-breach
A centre line between upper and lower control limits, with a point beyond a limit flashing red. Use for process control monitoring.
