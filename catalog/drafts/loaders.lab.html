.lab-ld-sklist

Skeleton Table
.lab-ld-sktable

.lab-ld-skprof

Ellipsis Loader
.lab-ld-ellipsis
The word Loading with dots that step from none to three and wrap, driven by an animated content string. Use for inline text-only waiting states.
Segmented Ring Count
.lab-ld-segring
Twelve ticks light up clockwise while the center counts n of 12, both driven by one registered property on the parent. Use for stepwise jobs with a known count.
Dots to Check
.lab-ld-dotcheck
Three hopping dots converge and merge, then a green check pops out of the merge point inside a ring. Use where a wait ends in a visible success.
Chasing Squares
.lab-ld-chase
Four squares of fading opacity hop clockwise around a two by two grid, each one step behind the last. Use as a compact indeterminate loader for panels.
Striped Progress Bar
.lab-ld-stripes
A determinate bar fills to full while a barber pole stripe keeps moving across the fill. Use for installs and copies where progress is known but slow.

Text Fill Loader

Flip Tiles
.lab-ld-fliptiles
Five tiles flip on their horizontal axis in sequence to a coloured back face, then flip on to the plain front. Use for board or grid views that are refreshing.
Diamond Ripple
.lab-ld-diamond

A round mouth opens and closes while a train of dots glides in from the right and vanishes at the jaws. Use for queue draining or batch consumption states.

Counting Percent
.lab-ld-count
A large number counts from 0 to 100 through an integer property and a CSS counter while a hairline bar fills in step. Use when the number is the whole message.
Caterpillar Pills
.lab-ld-pills
Four pills stretch wide and shrink back in sequence so the row crawls like a caterpillar. Use as a friendly inline loader inside buttons and chips.
Hex Map
.lab-ld-hexmap
Six outer hexes flip on their edges in a chase around a central hex, which fills from cyan to violet as the count climbs to 100%. A honeycomb progress loader.
Hex Orbit
.lab-ld-hexorbit
The hex map, but each outer hex flips on the edge that touches the central hex, so the ring rolls around it almost like a wheel while the centre fills to 100%.
