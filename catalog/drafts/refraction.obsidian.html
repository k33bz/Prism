.callout[data-callout-metadata*="refraction"]
Obsidian's own callout markup in Refraction: a beam of the callout color lands on the lit top edge, a lit index marks the side, the title becomes a mono instrument label with its wavelength, and hairline icons. In Obsidian, write > [!tip|refraction] and paste the CSS as a snippet — it then reads your theme's callout colors.
