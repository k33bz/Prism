# freefrontend idea batches

freefrontend.com is used as an idea catalogue only. Every facet here is rebuilt from scratch under
Prism's conventions (token colours, prefixed classes and keyframes, offline, reduced-motion, 4s loop).
No pen code is copied and no pen author is credited because none is reproduced.

Status: `todo` (not started), `drafted` (fragment written, not integrated), `landed` (in Prism.html,
catalog and showcase). Per-batch idea lists are the remaining work; strike ideas as they land.
