Live Avatar Stack (Status Rings)
.ns-avstack.ns-avstack-live
Stacked avatars with glowing status rings and an overflow chip whose ring splits by the hidden members' statuses. Visible slots = clamp(online, --max, ceil(--max × (1 + --expand))): six members, five online shows 5 / +1; two go away and offline, the stack folds back to 3 / +3 and the chip ring turns one third away, two thirds offline.
