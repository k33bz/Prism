BUTTON HOVER EFFECTS

Rainbow Border Sweep
.in-bfx-border
A gradient ring built from the six theme accents fades in around the button and slides on hover. The idle loop previews it, and the rainbow retints with the theme.
Conic Rainbow Ring
.in-bfx-conic
A conic gradient of the theme accents rotates behind the button, showing only as a bright spinning rim. It reveals on hover and previews on the idle loop.
Fill From Center
.in-bfx-center
A gradient disc grows from the centre to flood the button on hover. The classic radial call-to-action fill; the loop demonstrates it.
Fill From Left
.in-bfx-left
A gradient wipes in from the left edge to fill the button on hover, the classic sliding-fill call to action. Idle loop included.
Fill From Bottom
.in-bfx-up
The fill rises from the bottom edge to cover the button on hover, a rising-liquid take on the fill button.
Diagonal Fill
.in-bfx-diag
A slanted gradient sweeps across from the lower left to fill the button on hover. The angled cut gives it momentum.
Split Fill
.in-bfx-split
Two halves fill inward from the left and right edges and meet in the middle on hover, a shutter-style reveal.
Glow Pulse
.in-bfx-glow
The border and label light up and a layered glow blooms around the button on hover. Colour follows the role, so it re-tints. Idle loop previews it.
Shine Sweep
.in-bfx-shine
A diagonal band of light sweeps across the face of the button on hover, the glossy product-button gleam.
Gradient Text
.in-bfx-gtext
On hover the label takes a scrolling gradient of the theme accents while the button stays quiet, a subtle premium cue.
Neon Outline
.in-bfx-neon
The button goes transparent with a glowing outline and text shadow on hover, the arcade-sign look. Colour from the info role.
Lift and Glow
.in-bfx-lift
The button rises a few pixels and pools a soft accent glow beneath it on hover, a tactile raise. Idle loop nudges it.
CTA Arrow Slide
.in-bfx-cta
An arrow slides out from behind the label and the button tints on hover, the forward call to action. The loop shows the arrow appearing.
Gradient Shift
.in-bfx-shift
The button wears a theme-accent gradient that scrolls; on hover it scrolls faster and lifts a coloured shadow, a living primary button.
Ripple Expand
.in-bfx-ripple
A ring expands from the centre and the button tints on hover, the material-style ripple. The loop fires it periodically.
