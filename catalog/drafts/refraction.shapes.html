Refraction paths — light bent through letters

Beam-Traced Path
.ts-rf-beam (SVG textPath)
A hairline of white light runs along a curved <textPath>; the letters it passes light up in spectral order, violet to rose, then dim and the beam returns. One dash timeline drives the beam, its trail and a wide stroke mask, so the reveal is exact on any curve. In Day, the beam becomes violet ink. Reduced motion shows it fully lit.

Spectral Arc
.ts-rf-arc
A word fans out of a glass prism like dispersed light: each glyph rides its own faint ray and takes the next stop of the Refraction ramp, violet to rose. Letters converge into place with a short chromatic split. Pure CSS: angles come from --i, colors from --k. Reduced motion shows the finished arc.
