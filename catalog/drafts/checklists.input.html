CHECKLISTS & TASK LISTS

Persistent Checklist
.chk-list
A checklist whose items stagger in on load and animate a popping tick, a green fill and a sweeping strike-through as you check them. The chk-store initializer saves each item to localStorage (keyed by the list's data-store id) so ticks survive a reload. Without JS it still animates and toggles, just resets on reload.

Task List with Progress
.chk-task
The same animated checklist with a header count and a progress bar that fills as items complete; the title gets a check when the list is done. The chk-store initializer keeps the "n / m" count and bar in sync and persists every item to localStorage. Needs the initializer for the live count and persistence; static bar without it.
