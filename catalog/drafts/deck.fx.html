DECK & HAND

DECK
Draw from Deck
.dk-drawtop
The top card lifts off the deck at left and travels to your hand, so you can see exactly where the draw comes from. The face-down deck stays put.
DISCARD
Draw from Discard
.dk-drawdisc
A face-up card lifts off the discard pile at right and comes across to your hand, drawing from the discard rather than the deck.
DECK
Deal Out
.dk-deal
The deck deals a card out to each of four seats in turn, so the source is clear. (Deal In, over in Card Motion, arrives from the upper right; this one shows the deck it leaves.)
Fan a Hand
.dk-fan
A stack fans out into a neat arc and closes again, the cards spread in your hand.
Riffle & Bridge
.dk-riffle
Two halves lift, riffle toward each other and settle into one stack with a small bridge, the classic shuffle.
Overhand Shuffle
.dk-overhand
A packet is drawn off the top and dropped to the front over and over, the overhand or side shuffle.
Table Wash
.dk-wash
Cards spread flat across the table, swirl, and gather back into a stack, the wash you do before a deal.
