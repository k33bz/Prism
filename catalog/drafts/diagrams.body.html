FISHBONE (ISHIKAWA)

Simple Fishbone
.dgm-fish
The basic Ishikawa skeleton: the problem in the head, a spine that draws in, four flexible cause bones with sub-causes that fade in along them. The early-stage brainstorming form.
6M Fishbone
.dgm-fish (6M)
The manufacturing standard: Manpower, Method, Machine, Material, Measurement and Mother Nature, three bones above and three below the spine, environment picked out in green. Lean and Six Sigma defect analysis.
4S Fishbone
.dgm-fish (4S)
Four service-delivery bones, Surroundings, Suppliers, Systems and Skills, each with sub-causes. The healthcare, hospitality and support-desk form.
8P Fishbone
.dgm-fish (8P)
The marketing fishbone: Product, Price, Place, Promotion, People, Process, Physical Evidence and Performance, four bones a side, drawing in left to right. Customer-experience and service-quality reviews.
5M + E Fishbone
.dgm-fish (5M+E)
Five operational bones with Environment split out and boxed in green so external conditions are weighed separately from the process. Compliance reviews and quality audits.
Process Fishbone
.dgm-fish (process)
Bones are the workflow stages in order rather than cause types, and a packet rides the spine stage by stage toward the effect. Handoff failures and bottlenecks.
Service Fishbone
.dgm-fish (service)
Staff, Systems, Policies, Communication, Customer Expectations and Service Environment: the customer-interaction bones for low satisfaction scores.
Healthcare Fishbone
.dgm-fish (clinical)
People, Procedures, Equipment, Policy, Environment and Communication under a cross-marked head: the clinical safety and patient-care form.
Root Cause Trace
.dgm-fish .trace
One sub-cause is flagged and a pulse travels from it along the bone, down the spine and into the head, showing the causal path a 5-Whys session would follow.

FLOW & LOGIC

Flowchart
.dgm-flow
Terminator, process box, diamond decision and the yes/no branches, with the connectors drawing in and a token riding the happy path. The workflow primitive.
Feedback Loop
.dgm-loop
Four steps around a ring, starting at 12 o'clock, with an arc and an orbiting marker circling Plan, Do, Check, Act forever. Any cyclical process.
Decision Tree
.dgm-tree
Diamond decisions branching into outcomes, the chosen path highlighted as it draws and the winning leaf popping in green. Option analysis and playbooks.
Swimlane Flow
.dgm-lane
Three role lanes with a process that crosses them and a token following the handoffs, so ownership at each step reads at a glance.
Cycle Diagram
.dgm-parts (cycle)
Four wedge segments around a hub, each lighting in turn clockwise with small direction ticks between them. Iterative and lifecycle processes.
State Machine
.dgm-state
Rounded states with labelled transitions; the active state lights in sequence Idle, Running, Paused, Done as if the machine were stepping. Lifecycle and protocol docs.
Chevron Process
.dgm-chev
Five interlocking chevrons that fill progressively left to right, the classic phased-process strip with a timing row underneath.
SIPOC
.dgm-sipoc
Suppliers, Inputs, Process, Outputs, Customers as five columns with the process column emphasised and a flow running left to right beneath them. The Six Sigma scoping view.

HIERARCHY & STRUCTURE

Mind Map
.dgm-mind
A central idea with four coloured branches growing outward into nodes and second-level twigs, each level drawing in after the last. Brainstorms and topic maps.
Tree / Dendrogram
.dgm (tree)
A ranked hierarchy built top-down: each level's connectors draw and its nodes pop in after the parent level, ending on a highlighted leaf. Taxonomies and org-like structures.
Org Chart (horizontal)
.dgm (org)
Reporting lines left to right: a director, three managers and their teams with headcounts, the elbow connectors drawing before each column pops in.
Pyramid Chart
.dgm-pyr
Four tiers stack up from the base, each rising into place with the colour intensifying toward the peak. Ranked building blocks and hierarchies of need.
Funnel Chart
.dgm-funnel
Four narrowing stages with counts and conversion rates on the side, revealed top to bottom with a drip falling out of the spout. Sales and marketing funnels.
Concentric Circles
.dgm-conc
Nested rings from core outward with a ripple expanding through them, the layered-hierarchy form of a circle diagram.
Circle of Parts
.dgm-parts
Six equal wedges around a hub, spinning into place one after another so no part outranks another. Values, pillars, and other equal-weight sets.
Nested Containers
.dgm-nest
System, subsystem, component, module as boxes inside boxes, the highlight stepping inward level by level. Scope and containment diagrams.

COMPARISON & MATRIX

Venn (2 sets)
.dgm-venn
Two translucent circles breathing against each other with the overlap labelled and blinking, where similarities and differences carry no rank.
Venn (3 sets)
.dgm-venn (3)
Three overlapping sets with the triple intersection marked and called out, the desirable, feasible, viable framing.
SWOT Grid
.dgm-swot
Four equal quadrants, green, red, blue and amber, filling in order with their big letters and bullet points. Strategic positioning at a glance.
Priority Quadrant
.dgm-quad
Two labelled axes making four zones, items plotting in one by one and the upper-right zone pulsing as the priority corner. Eisenhower and impact/effort matrices.
PEST Columns
.dgm (pest)
Political, Economic, Social and Technological as four colour-coded columns popping in with their factors. The macro-environment scan.
Balance Scale
.dgm-scale
A beam scale that tips toward the heavier pan and settles back, the pros-versus-cons metaphor for two-sided comparisons.
Continuum Slider
.dgm-cont
Two markers on a gradient continuum sliding toward each other and back, for positioning items between two poles rather than in boxes.

TIMELINE & PLANNING

Journey Map
.dgm-journey
Stages across the top and an emotion curve drawing beneath them, with a touchpoint and mood at each stage. The customer or user experience over time.
Roadmap
.dgm-road
A quarter-by-quarter rail with dated milestones that light up as the progress bar reaches them. Product and strategic planning with benchmarks.
Vertical Timeline
.dgm-tl
Events alternate left and right of a central spine, each dot and card appearing in date order. Histories, changelogs and project retrospectives.
Gantt + Dependencies
.dgm-gd
Owner-labelled task bars on a week grid with dashed dependency links drawing between them and a milestone diamond at the end. Delivery planning with accountability.
Milestone Track
.dgm-run
A runner moves along the track pausing at each flag, and every flag it passes turns green. Progress toward staged goals.
PERT / Critical Path
.dgm-pert
Activity nodes and duration edges drawing in, with the critical path picked out in red and totalled beneath. Schedule risk at a glance.
Value Stream Map
.dgm-vsm
Process boxes with cycle times, flowing arrows and wait times between them, and the lead-time ladder underneath separating value-add from waiting. Lean flow analysis.

NETWORK & RELATIONSHIPS

Network Graph
.dgm-net
Nodes drifting gently on their springs with edges between them, the busy edges carrying a flowing dash. Service maps and relationship webs.
Hub & Spoke
.dgm-hub
A central hub with six spokes and pulses streaming outward along each one in turn. Distribution, integration and broadcast topologies.
Sankey Flow
.dgm-sankey
Three sources feeding two targets through bands whose width carries the volume, with dashed streams flowing through them. Traffic, budget and energy flows.
ER Diagram
.dgm-er
Two entities with header bands and attribute rows joined by a crow's-foot one-to-many relation that carries a flowing pulse. Data modelling.
Chord Diagram
.dgm-chord
Four groups on a slowly turning ring with chords between them showing who works with whom. Inter-team and inter-region relationships.
Stakeholder Map
.dgm-stake
A power/interest grid with the four engagement zones, stakeholder bubbles growing into place sized by influence. Communication planning.

FRAMEWORKS

Business Model Canvas
.dgm-bmc
The nine-block canvas with each block lighting in the order a workshop fills it, partners through revenue. Strategy on one page.
OKR Tree
.dgm-okr
One objective cascading into three key results with progress bars that fill to their current score, and the initiatives beneath each. Goal alignment.
5 Whys Chain
.dgm-whys
Each answer zig-zags down to the next "why?" until the root cause card lands in red. The companion to a fishbone session.
RACI Matrix
.dgm-raci
Tasks against roles with the R, A, C and I letters popping into their cells in colour. Responsibility assignment.
Five Forces
.dgm-p5
Rivalry at the centre with entrants, substitutes, suppliers and buyers around it, each arrow pressing inward in turn. The industry-analysis framework.
